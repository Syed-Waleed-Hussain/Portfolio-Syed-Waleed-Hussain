Transforming chaos into clarity through intelligent software & scalable data systems.

My journey moved from theoretical learning to building real-world impact. These projects weren't just academic assignments; they were proof to myself that I can solve complex data handling problems and build systems that work.

I am not just looking for a job; I am looking for the next challenge where I can apply my skills in Generative AI, RAG, and Computer Vision to help companies build products that matter.

I’m currently completed my 6th semester and hungry for the next big project.
